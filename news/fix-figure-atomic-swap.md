- A saved figure now replaces the earlier one by a rename within its folder, after a copy beside it under a hidden name, so a copy that fails on a full disk or a dropped share leaves the earlier figure whole rather than empty. A figure that draws nothing writes no blank PDF or TIFF for the publisher and removes the earlier render's copies, with a warning whether or not the report embeds it. A figure saved twice in one run that draws nothing the second time is not recorded as an exhibit, the placeholder for an embedded blank page is staged like any figure, and a temporary folder that cannot be made is named.
