* Docs sweep before 1.6.2. The version-floor block in seventeen templates (rf, rhf, sid, vp and vt) no longer claims the floors were "verified against on <date>", a date several had outgrown when a floor was raised; it now says they are the oldest versions the template works with, and each floor keeps its own reason (#582). `rfs-fit` says "events" rather than "deaths" in its narration, captions and learning-curve table, since its endpoint is any time-to-event outcome (#583). `rfc-fit`, `rfr-fit` and `rfs-fit` drop learning-curve simulation figures that had no source and keep the guidance they illustrated (#579). `template_catalog()` documents that `uses` names the functions a template is built on rather than every call, and `hp` and `hs-setup` no longer list functions they do not call (#584).
