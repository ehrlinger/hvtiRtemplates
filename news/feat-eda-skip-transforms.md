* `dc-eda` takes `TRANSFORMS`. `FALSE`, the default, draws one form of each variable a variable set reaches, the plain one, and lists the transforms a data build adds beside it (`ln_age`, `in_age`, `age2`) as not drawn; `TRUE` draws them all. A column named in `VARIABLES` is drawn either way. The forms are grouped by `hvtiRutilities::concept_map()`, the rule the bootstrap screens use. At the 2026-11-11 Biostats training `VARIABLE_SET <- "all"` drew a page per transform and took six minutes.
