* `dc-eda`'s `VARIABLE_SET` stacks variable sets, in the order named: `c("ods", "mitral", "redcap")` draws the house ODS list, then the study's mitral set, then its REDCap fields, each column once, at its first set's place, and the report counts each set's columns found. The house modules are the blocks the SAS ODS template carries commented out: `"ie"`, `"pericardiectomy"`, `"cabg"` and `"valve"`. Its Surgeon block is not one, its columns being named for people. A study declares its own sets under `variable_sets:` in `_study.yml`, as a list of columns or as sections each listing columns, and the template shows how to list a REDCap project's fields from its data dictionary. `"all"` still draws every column, on its own.
